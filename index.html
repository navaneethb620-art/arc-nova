Who we are

Design ideas, made easier to visualize.

ARC-NOVA is an emerging 3D visualization studio focused on transforming interior design concepts and visual references into three-dimensional environments.

We aim to support architects and interior designers by converting design ideas into visual 3D representations that make spaces easier to understand, explore and present.

Our approach combines creativity and technology to bridge the gap between traditional design references and three-dimensional visualization.

What we offer

Visualization services for design professionals.

Project-based 3D support shaped around the drawings, references and requirements you provide.

01 / CONVERSION

2D-to-3D Conversion

Transform supplied interior drawings, layouts and visual references into three-dimensional representations.

02 / INTERIORS

Interior 3D Visualization

Recreate interior elements such as wardrobes, TV units, study areas, bedrooms, living rooms and ceiling concepts in 3D.

03 / WALKTHROUGH

3D Walkthrough Preview

Explore a prepared 3D environment through a navigable walkthrough and view the proposed space from different viewpoints.

04 / INTERACTION

Interactive Furniture Arrangement

Use supported drag-and-drop controls to move and arrange selected furniture and interior elements in the 3D environment.

Feature availability and project scope are confirmed before work begins. VR headset-based presentation is a future development and is not offered as a current service.
